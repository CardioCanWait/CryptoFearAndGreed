Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Crypto Fear & Greed is a lightweight app that shows the market sentiment index. We built it to be simple and respectful of your data.

Scope: platforms and stores

This policy applies to every version of the app, on every platform and from every distribution channel: the iOS app (Apple App Store, TestFlight and other Apple distribution), and the Android app from Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, and any other store or direct download. It also covers the app's widgets, notifications and background refresh, and the website where this policy is published.

Not every version uses every service described below. Which services a version uses depends on its platform and store. As a rule:

  • iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions use Google services: Firebase (analytics, crash reports, remote configuration), Google AdMob (advertising) and Google's consent form.
  • The Huawei AppGallery version does not use Google services. It uses HUAWEI Ads and its own consent notice; see Huawei AppGallery version.
  • Future versions and stores may add or change services within the limits described in Features and services we may add.

We prefer to describe services that a version may use, now or soon, so that this policy stays accurate as the app grows. Where this policy says a version "may" do something, that version might not do it yet.

What the app collects

The app does not require accounts, names, email addresses, or other personal information, and it does not ask you to type anything about yourself. It requests the current sentiment index and market data from the services listed under Third-party services and servers over the internet.

Depending on the version and on your choices, the app may also collect limited usage and crash information, receive remote settings, and show ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

To make the app feel fast, it saves the latest index and market values, recent history, your settings, widget settings, alert rules and thresholds, your consent and privacy choices, and refresh timing locally on your device. Widgets on iOS and Android read some of this data from storage shared with the app on your device. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded by the app. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

Usage analytics and crash reports

The iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They help us understand which features are used, whether data loads successfully, and why the app crashes, so we can fix problems and improve the app. Other versions or stores may use similar analytics or crash reporting services, including the Huawei equivalents (such as HUAWEI Analytics Kit and AppGallery Connect Crash), and we may add other Firebase measurement features such as performance monitoring.

This information may include:

  • Usage events, such as screens viewed, whether a data refresh succeeded or failed (with a technical reason), which chart range was loaded, whether ads were requested or shown, how you used widgets and notifications, and how you answered the app's support and review prompts.
  • App and device details, such as app version, operating system version, device model, language, and the store the app was installed from.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics, such as the technical error and the state of the app when it failed.
  • Identifiers created by Google or the platform for the app installation (such as a Firebase installation ID), and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.

We do not collect your name, email address, account details, or anything you type, and we do not use this information to identify you. We do not sell it.

Remote configuration

The iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions use Google's Firebase Remote Config. Other versions or stores may use an equivalent service (for example HUAWEI AppGallery Connect Remote Configuration). Remote configuration lets us adjust app settings, such as how often ads and review prompts may appear, how long widgets stay unlocked, and the addresses of the servers the app contacts, and switch features on or off without an app update. The app fetches these settings in the background and applies them the next time it starts.

To deliver the settings, the app sends the service provider its country, language, time zone, operating system version, app and bundle identifiers, SDK version, and an installation ID. With Google Analytics, user properties and the first-open time may be used to target settings.

This information is not used for personalization, advertising, or to identify you. Remote configuration works regardless of your analytics and advertising choices, because the app needs it to receive its settings.

Huawei AppGallery version

The version of the Android app distributed through Huawei AppGallery does not use Google services. At the time of writing it does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, or Google AdMob, and it does not send us usage analytics or crash reports. It uses built-in default settings instead of remote configuration. We may add Huawei's own equivalents of these services to it; if we do, they work as described in the analytics, crash report and remote configuration sections above, with Huawei as the service provider, and consent is asked where the law requires it.

Ads. This version shows ads served by HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) through the HUAWEI Ads Kit software development kit. Huawei's Ads SDK privacy statement, which Huawei states applies to devices sold in mainland China, says the SDK may process device identifiers (such as the Open Advertising Identifier, OAID), device model and hardware information, app and usage information, network information and IP address, and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks), to display, measure, and secure ads and to prevent fraud. Similar data may be processed for users in other regions. This app does not request permission to access your device's location. Huawei may still derive an approximate location from network information such as your IP address. We do not receive your personal data from HUAWEI Ads; we only see aggregated ad performance reports, such as impressions and revenue. Huawei processes this data under its own terms and privacy notices and may process it on servers in other countries; see Third-party services and servers below.

Consent for ads. The first time the app starts, it shows a notice that names HUAWEI Ads as the recipient of your device identifiers (such as the OAID) and network information and asks you to allow or decline. The app does not start the HUAWEI Ads SDK, request ads, or give HUAWEI Ads access to these identifiers before you answer, and it shows no ads if you decline or have not answered yet; the rest of the app works the same. The notice appears for users in every region. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, your answer is the legal basis for showing ads. If you allow ads, the app asks HUAWEI Ads for personalized ads; if you decline, no ads are requested at all. Separately, if you turn off personalized ads in your device's system settings, Huawei sets your OAID to zeros, which the app does not override. The app does not mark its audience as children and it is not directed at children. The app does not read or store your OAID itself.

Your choices. You can review and change your answer at any time through Privacy settings in the app; the notice is shown again and your new answer replaces the old one. Declining or withdrawing stops new ad requests, and the ad is hidden. Your answer is stored only on your device. On Huawei devices you can also reset your OAID or turn off personalized ads in the device's system settings. The Google consent form described below applies to the other versions of the app.

App reviews. If you choose to rate the app, the rating prompt is handled by Huawei AppGallery, and the app's AppGallery listing may open instead if the prompt is unavailable. What you submit there is handled by Huawei.

Your choices and consent (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions)

This section applies to the iOS app and the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store, and to any other version that uses Google's consent form. It does not apply to the Huawei AppGallery version, whose choices are described in the Huawei section above.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, these versions ask for your consent in a form provided by Google when you first open them. Analytics and crash reporting are enabled only if you consent to analytics. If you decline, analytics collection and crash-report uploads are disabled. Ad availability and personalisation depend on your advertising consent and the ad provider’s requirements.

In other regions, analytics and crash reporting are on by default, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and improving it. You can switch them off at any time.

In either case, you can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings in the app. On Android, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. On iOS, see the tracking permission described under Advertising.

Remote configuration, described above, is not covered by these choices. It works regardless of your analytics and advertising choices because the app needs it to receive its settings. Basic technical requests that any app makes to the servers it needs, such as to load market data, are also not covered by them.

Advertising

The iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions show ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID or, with permission on iOS, the advertising identifier), IP address, approximate location, and usage and diagnostic data to provide, measure, and secure ads. Ads may include banners, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to unlock a feature for a limited time. Where required, the ad consent form described above applies. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob. The Huawei AppGallery version shows ads served by HUAWEI Ads, described above. We may add or change advertising partners or ad mediation services; any such partner will be listed under Third-party services and servers before or when it is first used, and ads are not requested where your consent is required and has not been given.

On iOS, Apple separately asks whether you allow tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising and measurement. You can decline and still use the app. You can change this permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. This permission is separate from your analytics choice in the app’s Privacy settings.

Notifications, widgets and background refresh

The app can show alerts and notifications, for example when the sentiment index crosses a threshold you set. Alert rules and thresholds that you create stay on your device. To check them, the app periodically requests the public Fear & Greed and market data in the background, using the background refresh features of iOS and Android. Notifications are created on your device, and the app asks for your permission where your operating system requires it. No alert settings, device tokens, or personal information are sent to us for this. Widgets show data that the app has already stored on your device.

If we later add notifications that are sent from a server (push notifications), the app will share a device or push token with the push service of your platform (Apple, Google or Huawei) and with us, only to deliver those notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Third-party services and servers

The app interacts with these external services:

  • A public Fear & Greed index data API (alternative.me) for sentiment data. Like any web service, it can see your device's IP address and basic request details when the app contacts it.
  • Our market data server, which provides coin lists, global market statistics and price history to the app. It is hosted on Google Cloud (Cloud Run) and, like any web server, may process your device's IP address and basic request details, for example in server logs, to deliver and protect the service. We do not use it to identify you. Its address can be changed through remote configuration.
  • Our static crypto project information site, which provides project descriptions and links shown in coin details. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and may process your device's IP address and basic request details in the same way. Its address can also be changed through remote configuration.
  • Google AdMob (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for advertising.
  • HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) and the HUAWEI Ads Kit (Huawei AppGallery version) for advertising.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for usage analytics and crash reports.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config and Firebase installations (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for remote app settings and installation identifiers.
  • Google's user consent form (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) to collect your consent where the law requires it.
  • Apple, Google, Samsung and Huawei, as the operators of the platform and store, for example for app reviews, notifications and purchases, under their own terms.
  • Other services listed above under Features and services we may add, if and when we add them.

Google processes this information as our service provider and under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Google may process data in countries outside your own, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

In the Huawei AppGallery version, advertising is provided by Huawei, which processes the resulting data under its own terms and privacy notices, including the legal basis for that processing, how long it keeps the data, and any safeguards for transfers to other countries. See Huawei's Ads SDK privacy statement, which Huawei states applies to devices sold in mainland China, and, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the data protection information for Ads Kit (EU) that Huawei publishes in its Ads Kit documentation.

How long data is kept

Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Firebase project, which is at most 14 months. Firebase installation IDs are kept until they are deleted at our request; Google removes them within 180 days after deletion. These periods apply to the services we use in the iOS app and the Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions. Data processed by HUAWEI Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version is kept under Huawei's own policies, and data processed by any other service we add is kept under that service's policies and its retention settings in our account. Server logs of our own servers are kept only as long as needed to run and protect them. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on where you live, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and US states with privacy laws such as California, you may have the right to ask about the data we hold, to have it deleted or corrected, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Because the app has no accounts, we generally cannot link analytics data to a particular person. We will still answer any request as far as we can. Contact us using the details below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Where the law treats personalized advertising as "sharing" or "targeted advertising", you can opt out in the app's Privacy settings, in your device's advertising settings, and, on iOS, through the tracking permission.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Security and international transfers

We use the security features of the platforms and services we rely on, and the app's data requests are sent over encrypted connections where the service supports them. The services above may process data in countries other than yours, under the safeguards named in their own terms and privacy notices.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. The services described here apply only to the versions that include them, as explained in the scope section. We update this policy before introducing materially different data uses.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Crypto Fear & Greed is a lightweight app that shows the market sentiment index. We built it to be simple and respectful of your data.

What the app collects

The app does not require accounts, names, email addresses, or other personal information, and it does not ask you to type anything about yourself. It requests the current sentiment index from a public API over the internet.

The iOS app and the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store collect limited usage and crash information, subject to your choices described below. Those Android versions also receive remote settings to keep the app working. The Huawei AppGallery version of the Android app works differently; see Huawei AppGallery version below.

Data stored on your device

To make the app feel fast, it saves the latest index value, recent history, widget settings, and refresh timing locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded by the app.

Usage analytics and crash reports (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions)

The iOS app and the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They help us understand which features are used, whether data loads successfully, and why the app crashes, so we can fix problems and improve the app.

This information may include:

  • Usage events, such as screens viewed, whether a data refresh succeeded or failed (with a technical reason), which chart range was loaded, whether ads were requested or shown, and how you answered the app's support and review prompts.
  • App and device details, such as app version, operating system version, device model, language, and the store the app was installed from.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics, such as the technical error and the state of the app when it failed.
  • Identifiers created by Google for the app installation (such as a Firebase installation ID), and an approximate location (country or region) derived by Google from your IP address.

We do not collect your name, email address, account details, or anything you type, and we do not use this information to identify you. We do not sell it.

Remote configuration (Android: Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions)

The Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store use Google's Firebase Remote Config. It lets us adjust app settings, such as how often ads and review prompts may appear and how long widgets stay unlocked, and switch features on or off without an app update. The app fetches these settings in the background and applies them the next time it starts.

To deliver the settings, the app sends Google its country, language, time zone, operating system version, app identifiers and SDK version, and a Firebase installation ID.

This information is not used for personalization, advertising, or to identify you, and it is not linked to your analytics choice. Remote configuration works regardless of that choice, because the app needs it to receive its settings.

Huawei AppGallery version

The version of the Android app distributed through Huawei AppGallery does not use Google services. It does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, or Google AdMob, and it does not send us usage analytics or crash reports. It uses built-in default settings instead of remote configuration.

Ads. This version shows ads served by HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) through the HUAWEI Ads Kit software development kit. Huawei's Ads SDK privacy statement, which Huawei states applies to devices sold in mainland China, says the SDK may process device identifiers (such as the Open Advertising Identifier, OAID), device model and hardware information, app and usage information, network information and IP address, and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks), to display, measure, and secure ads and to prevent fraud. Similar data may be processed for users in other regions. This app does not request permission to access your device's location. Huawei may still derive an approximate location from network information such as your IP address. We do not receive your personal data from HUAWEI Ads; we only see aggregated ad performance reports, such as impressions and revenue. Huawei processes this data under its own terms and privacy notices and may process it on servers in other countries; see the third-party services section below.

Consent for ads. The first time the app starts, it shows a notice that names HUAWEI Ads as the recipient of your device identifiers (such as the OAID) and network information and asks you to allow or decline. The app does not start the HUAWEI Ads SDK, request ads, or give HUAWEI Ads access to these identifiers before you answer, and it shows no ads if you decline or have not answered yet; the rest of the app works the same. The notice appears for users in every region. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, your answer is the legal basis for showing ads. If you allow ads, the app asks HUAWEI Ads for personalized ads; if personalization is not permitted, only non-personalized ads are requested. The app does not mark its audience as children and it is not directed at children. The app does not read or store your OAID itself.

Your choices. You can review and change your answer at any time through Privacy settings in the app; the notice is shown again and your new answer replaces the old one. Declining or withdrawing stops new ad requests, and the ad is hidden. Your answer is stored only on your device. On Huawei devices you can also reset your OAID or turn off personalized ads in the device's system settings. The Google consent form described below applies to the other versions of the app.

Alerts and widgets. Alert rules and thresholds that you create stay on your device. To check them, the app periodically requests the public Fear & Greed and market data described above in the background. No alert settings, device tokens, or personal information are sent to us.

App reviews. If you choose to rate the app, the rating prompt is handled by Huawei AppGallery, and the app's AppGallery listing may open instead if the prompt is unavailable. What you submit there is handled by Huawei.

Your choices and consent (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions)

This section applies to the iOS app and the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store. It does not apply to the Huawei AppGallery version, whose choices are described in the Huawei section above.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, these versions ask for your consent in a form provided by Google when you first open them. Analytics and crash reporting are enabled only if you consent to analytics. If you decline, analytics collection and crash-report uploads are disabled. Ad availability and personalisation depend on your advertising consent and the ad provider’s requirements.

In other regions, analytics and crash reporting are on by default, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and improving it. You can switch them off at any time.

In either case, you can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the app's main screen. On Android, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Remote configuration, described above, is not covered by this choice. It works regardless of your analytics choice because the app needs it to receive its settings.

Advertising

In the iOS app and the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store, the app shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID or, with permission on iOS, the advertising identifier), IP address, approximate location, and usage and diagnostic data to provide, measure, and secure ads. Where required, the ad consent form described above applies. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob.

On iOS, Apple separately asks whether you allow tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising and measurement. You can decline and still use the app. You can change this permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. This permission is separate from your analytics choice in the app’s Privacy settings.

Third-party services

The app interacts with these external services:

  • Public Fear & Greed index and market data API (alternative.me) for sentiment and market data. Like any web service, it can see your device's IP address and basic request details when the app contacts it.
  • Google AdMob (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for advertising.
  • HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) and the HUAWEI Ads Kit (Huawei AppGallery version) for advertising.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for usage analytics and crash reports.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config (Android: Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) for remote app settings.
  • Google's user consent form (iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions) to collect your consent where the law requires it.

Google processes this information as our service provider and under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Google may process data in countries outside your own, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

In the Huawei AppGallery version, advertising is provided by Huawei, which processes the resulting data under its own terms and privacy notices, including the legal basis for that processing, how long it keeps the data, and any safeguards for transfers to other countries. See Huawei's Ads SDK privacy statement, which Huawei states applies to devices sold in mainland China, and, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the data protection information for Ads Kit (EU) that Huawei publishes in its Ads Kit documentation.

How long data is kept

Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Firebase project, which is at most 14 months. Firebase installation IDs are kept until they are deleted at our request; Google removes them within 180 days after deletion. These periods apply to the services we use in the iOS app and the Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store versions. Data processed by HUAWEI Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version is kept under Huawei's own policies. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask about the data we hold, to have it deleted, or to object to its use. Because the app has no accounts, we generally cannot link analytics data to a particular person. We will still answer any request as far as we can. Contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. The analytics and crash reporting described here apply only to the versions that include these features. Remote configuration applies only to the Android versions distributed through Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store. We update this policy before introducing materially different data uses.

Contact

For questions about privacy or to make a request, please contact the developer through the contact details on the app's Google Play, Apple App Store, or Huawei AppGallery listing, or email cardiocanwait@gmail.com.
